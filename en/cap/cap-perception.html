Target tracking

Track 7 capstone projectPASSED

Result: The companion detects an object in video and makes the vehicle follow it in SITL/in the field via GUIDED/Offboard

What to submit

  • Camera → detector → command pipeline
  • Latency and FPS measurements
  • Demo in SITL with a simulated camera
  • Safety protocol: geofence, timeouts, pilot
  • Project engineering folder: requirements with IDs, what was tested and how, changelog (see the "Engineering Method 101" module)

Software alternative

A track's capstone project can be replaced with a project from the “UAS software” catalog — with the same submission requirements: criteria, automated tests, README.

  • Project 4: Detector + tracker + following in Gazebo
  • Project 5: Precision landing on a marker in simulation
  • Project 7: Photogrammetry on an open dataset

Track modules

  1. Cameras and video streaming on the companion
  2. Camera geometry: from a pixel to a point on the ground
  3. Computer vision: from OpenCV to neural networks
  4. Onboard ML: budget, quantization, evaluation, maintenance
  5. Visual navigation: optical flow, VIO, SLAM overview
  6. Mapping: surveying, georeferencing, photogrammetry
  7. Precision landing on a marker: ArUco, LANDING_TARGET, PLND
  8. Depth and obstacle avoidance: sensors, OBSTACLE_DISTANCE, algorithms
  9. Tracking and target-based control
Safety. Everything you can do in SITL or without props, you do there. Real flights only after ground tests, with a verified failsafe.
