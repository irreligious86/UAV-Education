CourseElective: INAVCapstone project

An INAV aircraft from scratch

Track 0 capstone projectPASSED

Result: A quadcopter or wing on INAV, set up from a blank board: a 5-waypoint mission and a tested failsafe with return to home

Expected result

  • Platform choice (quadcopter or wing) and a build sheet: weight, motors, battery, estimated flight time
  • The `diff all` configuration before and after setup, with a comment on every changed parameter
  • Calibration record: voltage and current vs. a multimeter and charger, compass, accelerometer (wing: CG, control surface throws)
  • SITL rehearsal of the setup: `diff all`, arming flags, restoring the configuration on a blank board; flight and RTH in SITL are optional (they require RealFlight or X-Plane)
  • Log of a real flight: position hold, a 5-waypoint mission, failsafe from a switched-off radio with return to home
  • A one-page setup card for your aircraft

Track modules

  1. Introduction to INAV
  2. GPS, compass and RTH in INAV
  3. INAV from scratch: a GPS quadcopter
  4. Дополнительные датчики INAV: дальномер и SURFACE, оптический поток, пито, температура
  5. Настройка мультиротора в INAV: PID, рейты, фильтры, EZ Tune и навигационные PID
  6. CLI INAV: все команды, diff/dump и профили, бэкап, восстановление и переезд 7 → 8 → 9
  7. INAV from scratch: a flying wing
  8. OSD в INAV: аналог и HD (DJI O3/O4, Walksnail, HDZero), раскладки, тревоги, свои элементы
  9. Подстройки в полёте INAV: adjrange, стик-команды, профили, OSD-меню
  10. Телеметрия и связь INAV: CRSF/SmartPort/LTM/MAVLink, MSP RC, Bluetooth и наземные станции
  11. INAV missions: waypoints and CLI wp
  12. Wing on INAV: mixer, auto launch, autotune
  13. Геозоны, safehome, trackback и автопосадка крыла в INAV
  14. Миксер INAV целиком: mmix/smix, выходы и таймеры, флапероны, подвесы, mixer profiles и VTOL
  15. INAV Programming Framework
  16. Свет, PINIO, пищалка и триггер камеры в INAV
