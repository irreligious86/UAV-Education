Log-based tuning

Track 3 capstone projectPASSED

Result: A/B profiles, before/after spectra, report with conclusions

What to submit

  • Baseline diff and profiles A/B/C/D
  • Logs for all profiles with session_notes
  • Before/after filter spectra in the motor band (dB)
  • Metrics table: motor noise, bounce-back, entry lag, peak tracking (medians)
  • Report: what was chosen and why
  • Project engineering folder: requirements with IDs, what was tested and how, changelog (see the "Engineering Method 101" module)
