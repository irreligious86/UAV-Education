Track modules

  1. Python for flight data analysis
  2. Signals and spectra: what the gyro sees
  3. Blackbox
  4. Mechanics before PID: motor balance from the log
  5. Filters
  6. ESC firmware: Bluejay, AM32, BLHeli_32 and ESC configuration
  7. RPM filter: the case of the "whistling" 10"
  8. Control theory: stability, margins, cascades
  9. System identification: chirp, frequency response, a model for tuning
  10. Data-driven PID tuning
  11. The A/B method: tuning as an experiment
  12. Rates, RC smoothing and feedforward: from the stick to the setpoint
  13. In-flight adjustments: the Adjustments tab, `adjrange` and the VTX on a switch
  14. The other Betaflight modes and features: turtle, launch control, 3D, acro trainer, Angle/Horizon, dynamic idle, camera control
  15. CLI and remap
  16. CLI automation over serial
  17. GPS in Betaflight: M8/M10 modules, configuration, accuracy and the home point
  18. Servos, PWM and timers: `resource SERVO`, `servo`, `smix`, SERVO_TILT and channel forwarding
  19. LEDs, the beeper and beacons: LED_STRIP, `led` / `color` / `mode_color`, beeper, DShot beacon
Safety. Everything you can do in SITL or without props, you do there. Real flights only after ground tests, with a verified failsafe.
