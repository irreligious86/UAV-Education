Track modules

  1. Introduction to Betaflight
  2. Ports and receiver
  3. SBUS, CRSF and UART inversion
  4. Modes and OSD
  5. Failsafe: stages and switch test
  6. ExpressLRS: packet rate, telemetry, LQ
  7. VTX tables and changing the video channel
  8. Digital FPV video: DJI, Walksnail, HDZero, OpenIPC
  9. Telemetry radios: SiK, RFD900, ELRS MAVLink, IP radios and LTE
  10. Weather and flight planning: wind, density altitude, cold, icing
  11. Field operations and teamwork
  12. Analysis of real crashes
  13. Maintenance and reliability: logs, battery life, maintenance schedule, MTBF
  14. Troubleshooting tree: the drone won't take off
Safety. Everything you can do in SITL or without props, you do there. Real flights only after ground tests, with a verified failsafe.
