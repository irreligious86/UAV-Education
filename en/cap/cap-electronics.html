CourseElectronics and radioCapstone project

Onboard electrical system, with calculations

Track 2 capstone projectPASSED

Result: The vehicle's power diagram with current and sag calculations, layout, multimeter and oscilloscope measurements, radio link budget

What to submit

  • Power diagram: buses, BECs, capacitors, fuses, wire gauges
  • Calculation of branch currents and sag under load
  • Measurements: voltages under load, ripple on the bus
  • Control and video link budget with antennas
  • Project engineering folder: requirements with IDs, what was tested and how, changelog (see the "Engineering Method 101" module)

Track modules

  1. Onboard power circuit design
  2. Measurements: multimeter, oscilloscope, logic analyzer
  3. Li-Ion and LiFePO4 packs: cells, welding, BMS
  4. Interference and electromagnetic compatibility
  5. Antennas, polarization and radiation patterns
  6. Radio link budget and range
  7. Connecting peripherals: UART, I2C, CAN in practice
  8. DroneCAN: the onboard CAN bus, nodes, ESCs and peripheral firmware
  9. Soldering, harnesses and connection reliability
  10. Onboard redundancy: X8, hexa, power and signals
  11. PCB design in KiCad: from schematic to order, layout for EMC
Safety. Everything you can do in SITL or without props, you do there. Real flights only after ground tests, with a verified failsafe.
