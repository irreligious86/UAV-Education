Track 6 capstone projectPASSED

Result: parameters, a SITL mission, a real flight, log analysis

What to submit

  • A parameter file with a date and the rationale for key decisions
  • A mission run in SITL on the target frame (octa-quad or quadplane)
  • Motor failure test in SITL (octo) or an output map and a ground transition test (VTOL)
  • A real flight log and its analysis: VIBE, ERR, ATT, BAT
  • Project engineering folder: requirements with IDs, what was tested and how, changelog (see the "Engineering Method 101" module)

Track modules

  1. Introduction to ArduPilot
  2. SITL: a drone without a drone
  3. ArduPilot с нуля: Copter через Mission Planner / QGC
  4. Simulation in Gazebo: worlds, sensors, a camera in ROS 2 and scenario tests
  5. Missions and planning
  6. Guidance and trajectories: pure pursuit, L1, S-curves, minimum snap
  7. ArduPilot logs: flight analysis
  8. Lua: scenarios and fault injection
  9. Heavy-lift platforms: X8 and octo
  10. Wing aerodynamics and plane flight modes
  11. QuadPlane and tilt-rotor VTOL
  12. ArduPilot tuning: AUTOTUNE, filters, ESC-based notch
  13. ArduPlane tuning: wing AUTOTUNE, TECS, L1 navigation, landing
  14. Fault tolerance: failsafe, geofence, flight FMEA
