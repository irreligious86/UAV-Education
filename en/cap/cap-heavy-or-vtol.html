An X8 octo with payload or a VTOL wing

Track 6 capstone projectPASSED

Result: parameters, a SITL mission, a real flight, log analysis

Expected result

  • A parameter file with a date and the rationale for key decisions
  • A mission run in SITL on the target frame (octa-quad or quadplane)
  • Motor failure test in SITL (octo) or an output map and a ground transition test (VTOL)
  • A real flight log and its analysis: VIBE, ERR, ATT, BAT
  • Project engineering folder: requirements with IDs, what was tested and how, changelog (see the "Engineering Method 101" module)

Track modules

  1. Introduction to ArduPilot
  2. SITL: a drone without a drone
  3. ArduPilot from scratch: Copter with Mission Planner / QGC
  4. OSD and video in ArduPilot: MAX7456, MSP DisplayPort (DJI, Walksnail, HDZero), OSDn screens, VTX
  5. ArduPilot outputs: SERVOn_FUNCTION, timer groups, DShot/BDShot, BLHeli, servos, GPIO and relays
  6. ArduPilot sensors on the bench: compass (MagFit, Large Vehicle), airspeed, several batteries
  7. LEDs and buzzer in ArduPilot: NTF_*, NeoPixel/ProfiLED, buzzer, DShot beacon
  8. Simulation in Gazebo: worlds, sensors, a camera in ROS 2 and scenario tests
  9. Missions and planning
  10. Guidance and trajectories: pure pursuit, L1, S-curves, minimum snap
  11. ArduPilot logs: flight analysis
  12. Lua: scenarios and fault injection
  13. Heavy-lift platforms: X8 and octo
  14. Wing aerodynamics and plane flight modes
  15. ArduPlane from scratch: servos, control surfaces, airspeed, wing failsafe
  16. QuadPlane inside: where the plane is and where the copter is
  17. QuadPlane and tilt-rotor VTOL
  18. VTOL modes and transitions in ArduPilot
  19. ArduPilot tuning: AUTOTUNE, filters, ESC-based notch
  20. Switches and knobs: RCx_OPTION, TUNE and in-flight tuning
  21. Payloads in ArduPilot: camera, gimbal, relays, drop and winch — wiring and parameters
  22. ArduPlane tuning: wing AUTOTUNE, TECS, L1 navigation, landing
  23. Fault tolerance: failsafe, geofence, flight FMEA
Safety. Everything you can do in SITL or without props, you do there. Real flights only after ground tests, with a verified failsafe.
