Exams

Exams work when accounts are enabled. Module quizzes are self-checks: their answers are in the public repository. Exams are different: questions and answers are stored only in the database, and the server does the grading.

Exams are available after you sign in — account.

Answers are saved in this browser — after a page reload, the attempt continues. When time runs out, the work is submitted automatically.
