Module score = 40% quiz (best attempt) + 60% lab; in modules with problems, 30% quiz + 30% problems + 40% lab. “Mastered” means quiz ≥ 80%, all problems solved and all lab items done. A manual mark shows as “marked”, and that's honestly a different thing.

Grader results

Course graders (examples/sitl-pymavlink/check_lab_log.py, examples/blackbox-grader/check_bf_log.py) with the flag --json result.json write a result file. Upload it — the lab items that the grader actually checked and that passed will be checked off automatically; in the table they are marked with “⚙”. This is a self-check: the file can be faked; it shows that the grader was run, not that anyone accepted the work.
