Flight and powertrain

Thrust-to-weight ratio
T/W = ΣTmax / (m·g)   [безразмерно; Tmax — максимальная тяга всех моторов, Н]

Hover throttle ≈ 1/(T/W). 2:1 for cargo, 3–4:1 for long range, 6+:1 for freestyle.

How a multirotor flies
Disk thrust (momentum theory)
F = ½·ρ·A·(Vₑ² − V₀²)   [Н; ρ ≈ 1.225 кг/м³, A = π·D²/4]

In hover V₀ = 0. A larger disk uses less power for the same thrust.

Propeller aerodynamics and power system selection
Propeller coefficients
Cₜ = T/(ρ·n²·D⁴)   Cₚ = P/(ρ·n³·D⁵)   J = V/(n·D)   η = J·Cₜ/Cₚ

n in rev/s, D in m. Consequence: thrust ~ n², power ~ n³.

Propeller aerodynamics and power system selection
Motor harmonic frequency
f₁ = RPM/60   fₖ = k·f₁   RPM = eRPM / (пары полюсов)

A motor with 14 magnets has 7 pole pairs. Needed for the RPM filter and for reading the spectrum.

RPM filter: the case of the "whistling" 10"
Flight time (estimate)
t [мин] = 60 · (C [А·ч] · k) / I [А]   k ≈ 0.8 — используемая доля ёмкости

I is the average current from the log, not the hover current on the bench.

Power: LiPo, Li-Ion, BEC
Internal resistance and voltage sag
U = E − I·Rвн   Rвн(пакет) = Rэл · S / P

6S4P Li-Ion at 30 mΩ per cell → 45 mΩ per pack → 2.7 V of sag at 60 A.

Onboard power circuit design
Motor torque constant
Kt = 60/(2π·KV)   [Н·м/А]

1750KV → 0.0055 N·m/A; 380KV → 0.025. Low KV means torque for big props.

Motors, ESCs and DShot
RPM under load
n ≈ KV·(U − I·R_обм)   [об/мин]

Sag from winding and battery resistance; heat in the winding P = I²·R.

Motors, ESCs and DShot
Wing lift
L = ½·ρ·V²·S·C_L   [Н]

In level flight L = m·g; thrust and drag work the same way, with C_D = C_D0 + k·C_L².

Wing aerodynamics and plane flight modes
Stall speed
V_s = √(2·m·g / (ρ·S·C_Lmax));  V_s(φ) = V_s/√cos φ   [м/с]

C_Lmax ≈ 1.0–1.3 without high-lift devices; 60° bank → +41%. AIRSPEED_MIN ≥ 1.2–1.3 V_s.

Wing aerodynamics and plane flight modes
Prop tip speed
v = π·D·n   [м/с; D в м, n в об/с]

5" at 14,000 rpm ≈ 93 m/s (335 km/h).

Safety and the preflight check

Electricity

Ohm's law and power
U = I·R   P = U·I = I²·R = U²/R   [В, А, Ом, Вт]

Heat in a linear BEC: P = (Vin − Vout)·I.

Onboard power circuit design
Wire resistance
R = ρ·L/S   ρ(медь) = 0.0172 Ом·мм²/м   ΔU = I·R (туда и обратно — 2L)

14 AWG ≈ 2.08 mm² → 8.3 mΩ/m; 12 AWG ≈ 3.31 mm² → 5.2 mΩ/m.

Onboard power circuit design
Inductive spike
U = L·dI/dt   L(провод) ≈ 1 мкГн/м

Why you need a low-ESR capacitor on the power pads: it absorbs the dI/dt from the ESC.

Onboard power circuit design
Magnetic field of a wire
B = μ₀·I/(2π·r)   μ₀ = 4π·10⁻⁷ Гн/м   → B[мкТл] ≈ 0.2·I[А]/r[м]

60 A at 5 cm gives 240 µT versus ~50 µT for Earth's field: a compass next to a power wire is useless.

Interference and electromagnetic compatibility
Decibels
дБ = 10·log₁₀(P₂/P₁)   дБм = 10·log₁₀(P/1 мВт)   +3 дБ ≈ ×2, +10 дБ = ×10

20 dBm = 100 mW, 27 dBm = 500 mW, 30 dBm = 1 W.

Radio link budget and range
Pack resistance
R_пак = R_эл·S / P + R_соединений   [Ом]

6S4P at 25 mΩ ≈ 38 mΩ + strip/connectors; sag ΔU = I·R_pack.

Li-Ion and LiFePO4 packs: cells, welding, BMS
Heat in a pack / wire
P = I²·R   [Вт]

60 A through 45 mΩ is 160 W; a 0.6 m 12 AWG wire at 50 A dissipates 7.8 W.

Li-Ion and LiFePO4 packs: cells, welding, BMS
Voltage spike from wire inductance
U = L·dI/dt   [В; L ≈ 1 мкГн/м]

ESC switching → spikes on the bus; a low-ESR capacitor on the pads.

Radio link

Free-space path loss
FSPL[дБ] = 20·log₁₀(d[км]) + 20·log₁₀(f[МГц]) + 32.44

2.4 GHz, 10 km → 120 dB. Doubling the range = +6 dB.

Radio link budget and range
Link budget
Pпр = Pпд + Gпд + Gпр − Lкаб − FSPL − Lдоп   M = Pпр − Sчувств ≥ 10–15 дБ

Sensitivity for ELRS 2.4 GHz: 50 Hz −115, 150 Hz −112, 250 Hz −108, 500 Hz −105 dBm.

Radio link budget and range
Wavelength
λ = c/f   λ/4: 915 МГц — 8.2 см, 2.4 ГГц — 3.1 см, 5.8 ГГц — 1.3 см

The antenna size must match the band.

Antennas, polarization and radiation patterns
ELRS telemetry interval
Tтлм[мс] = ratio · 1000 / packet_rate

1:128 at 250 Hz → 512 ms between telemetry frames.

ExpressLRS: packet rate, telemetry, LQ
FSPL
FSPL = 20·log₁₀(d_км) + 20·log₁₀(f_МГц) + 32.44   [дБ]

2.4 GHz, 10 km → 120 dB; +6 dB per doubling of range; 915→2400 MHz = +8.4 dB.

Radio link budget and range
VSWR losses
отражено = ((КСВ−1)/(КСВ+1))²

SWR 1.5 → 4% (−0.18 dB), 2.0 → 11% (−0.5 dB), 3.0 → 25% (−1.25 dB).

Signals, filters, control

Nyquist frequency
f_N = f_s / 2   — всё выше f_N отражается (алиасинг)

An 8 kHz gyro sees up to 4 kHz; analog anti-aliasing is in the sensor itself.

Filters
First-order filter (PT1)
|H(f)| = 1/√(1 + (f/f_c)²)   фазовое запаздывание φ = −arctan(f/f_c)   групповая задержка τ ≈ 1/(2π·f_c)

f_c = 100 Hz → delay ≈ 1.6 ms at low frequencies. Every filter adds delay to the loop.

Filters
Notch filter
центр f₀, ширина по −3 дБ Δf = f₀/Q   полоса от f₀ − Δf/2 до f₀ + Δf/2

Q = 10 at 250 Hz → 25 Hz bandwidth.

RPM filter: the case of the "whistling" 10"
PID controller
u(t) = Kp·e + Ki·∫e dt + Kd·de/dt (+ Kff·setpoint')

In Betaflight, D is taken from the measurement (gyro), not from the error, and is filtered separately.

PID controller in Betaflight
Transient response metrics
перерегулирование = (y_max − y_∞)/y_∞ · 100%   время установления: |y − y_∞| < 2–5%   задержка: время до 30–50% y_∞

Computed from the Blackbox step response; this is the language used to compare tunes.

Data-driven PID tuning
Complementary filter
θ = α·(θ + ω·dt) + (1 − α)·θ_acc   α = τ/(τ + dt)

Gyro for short time scales, accelerometer for long ones. τ = 1 s, dt = 1 ms → α ≈ 0.999.

Flight controller and sensors
PT1 group delay
τ ≈ 1/(2π·f_c)   [с]

100 Hz → 1.6 ms, 250 Hz → 0.64 ms; PT2 ×1.6, PT3 ×2.2.

Signals and spectra: what the gyro sees
Phase from latency
Δφ = −360°·f·T_d

5 ms at 50 Hz → −90°. A phase margin of 45–60° is a good tune.

Control theory: stability, margins, cascades
Overshoot from damping
overshoot = exp(−π·ζ/√(1−ζ²))

ζ 0.7 → 4.6%, 0.5 → 16%, 0.3 → 37%; settling time ≈ 4/(ζ·ω_n).

Control theory: stability, margins, cascades
Kalman gain (scalar)
K = P⁻/(P⁻ + R);  x = x⁻ + K·(z − h(x⁻));  P = (1−K)·P⁻

P is the estimate uncertainty, R the measurement noise; y²/S > gate means reject.

The Kalman filter in plain terms, and the EKF
Velocity from optical flow
v = (flow − ω)·h   [м/с]

Requires rangefinder altitude and gyro angular rate.

Altitude and position without GPS: baro, rangefinder, optical flow
Pressure altitude (near the ground)
Δh ≈ −8.4 м на 1 гПа

Dynamic pressure ½ρv² at 20 m/s ≈ 2.4 hPa ≈ 20 m of error without baro protection.

Altitude and position without GPS: baro, rangefinder, optical flow
Natural frequency of a beam
f₁ ≈ 0.56·√(E·I/(μ·L⁴))   [Гц]

Stiffer/shorter → higher; mass at the tip → lower. In practice, measure it with a tap.
