Glossary

185 terms. Each one links to the module where it's explained in detail.

ADR

Architecture Decision Record: context, options, decision, consequences.

Systems engineering: requirements, FMEA, documentation, tests

Aliasing

Frequencies above fs/2 showing up at false frequencies after sampling; fixed with an anti-aliasing filter and full-rate logging.

Signals and spectra: what the gyro sees

AM32

Open-source firmware for 32-bit ARM ESCs (STM32, AT32, GD32, etc.), a replacement for BLHeli_32.

ESC firmware: Bluejay, AM32, BLHeli_32 and ESC configuration

AP_DDS

The eProsima Micro XRCE-DDS client built into ArduPilot: publishes state to ROS 2 and accepts commands without MAVLink; on the ROS 2 side it is served by an agent (micro_ros_agent or Micro XRCE-DDS Agent).

ROS 2 + ArduPilot: AP_DDS and MAVROS, one node, two routes

AP_Periph

ArduPilot firmware for DroneCAN peripheral nodes: GPS, compass, airspeed sensor, ESC adapter on a separate STM32.

DroneCAN: the onboard CAN bus, nodes, ESCs and peripheral firmware

ArduPilot

An open-source autopilot: Copter, Plane, Rover, Sub. Navigation, missions, Lua, companion.

Introduction to ArduPilot

Arming disable flags

Betaflight flags that prevent arming (RXLOSS, THROTTLE, ANGLE, MSP…). Visible via status and in the OSD.

Safety and the preflight check

AUTOTUNE

Automatic tuning of rate/angle gains from a series of "twitches" on an axis in hover.

ArduPilot tuning: AUTOTUNE, filters, ESC-based notch

BEC

An onboard voltage regulator (5/9/12 V) for the FC, receiver, camera and VTX.

Power: LiPo, Li-Ion, BEC

Bias

The sensor output at zero input; for a gyro it integrates into attitude drift; estimated by the EKF.

IMU sensors: physics, errors, calibration

Bidirectional DShot

DShot with a return channel: the ESC reports motor eRPM. The basis of the RPM filter.

RPM filter: the case of the "whistling" 10"

Blackbox

Betaflight log: gyro, setpoint, PID, motors, RC at a high rate, for post-flight analysis.

Blackbox

Bluejay

Open-source firmware for ESCs on 8-bit EFM8 BB21 and BB51 (BB1 only up to version 0.18.1), the successor to BLHeli_S, with bidirectional DShot for the RPM filter.

ESC firmware: Bluejay, AM32, BLHeli_32 and ESC configuration

BMS

Battery management system: voltage/current/temperature protection and balancing; cutting off discharge in flight is dangerous.

Li-Ion and LiFePO4 packs: cells, welding, BMS

Bounce-back

Gyro rebound in the opposite direction after the stick stops; a metric of underdamping.

Data-driven PID tuning

C rating

A battery's rated discharge current as a multiple of its capacity. Usually inflated; trust the log and the internal resistance.

Power: LiPo, Li-Ion, BEC

Camera matrix K

Camera intrinsics: focal lengths f_x, f_y in pixels and the principal point c_x, c_y. Maps a ray to a pixel and back; found by checkerboard calibration.

Camera geometry: from a pixel to a point on the ground

CAN arbitration

Resolving simultaneous transmission: a dominant 0 wins, the lower ID wins without losing time.

UART, I2C, SPI, CAN at the signal and driver level

Cascaded controller

Nested loops: position commands velocity, velocity commands angle, angle commands angular rate. The inner loop is tuned first and made several times faster than the outer one.

PX4 control loops and tuning

Chirp

A test signal whose frequency rises smoothly from f₀ to f₁. The response to it is used to build the loop's frequency response.

System identification: chirp, frequency response, a model for tuning

Circular polarization

The field rotates (RHCP/LHCP); less sensitive to orientation and reflections; never mix RHCP and LHCP.

Antennas, polarization and radiation patterns

Clamp meter

An instrument that measures current from the magnetic field around a wire without breaking the circuit. DC requires a Hall-effect clamp.

Measurements: multimeter, oscilloscope, logic analyzer

Cloud Build

Building Betaflight firmware on the server for a selected set of options (defines).

Your own firmware builds

Coherence

A measure from 0 to 1 of how much of the output is linearly explained by the input at a given frequency. Below ~0.6, a point on the frequency response is not worth using.

System identification: chirp, frequency response, a model for tuning

Cold solder joint

A cold solder joint: dull, lumpy, with poor wetting. Holds on the bench and fails under vibration.

Soldering, harnesses and connection reliability

COMMAND_ACK

MAVLink command acknowledgment with a result. A command without an ACK check is hope, not control.

MAVLink and MAVSDK

Companion computer

An onboard computer for vision, scenarios, networking. It advises via GUIDED; the FC decides.

Companion: why, and where the boundary is

Complementary filter

Fusing the gyro (high frequencies) with the accelerometer/compass (low frequencies) using fixed coefficients; a special case of the Kalman filter.

Attitude: Euler angles, quaternions, complementary filter

Component ID

A component's number within a vehicle in MAVLink (autopilot 1, camera 100, gimbal 154, etc.). Together with the system ID it forms the address.

Your own MAVLink component: sensor, parameters, messages

Conducted interference

Noise conducted along power and ground wires (ripple from ESCs and converters), not through the air. Fixed with an LC filter and a low-ESR capacitor at the source.

Interference and electromagnetic compatibility

Config diff

A line-by-line comparison of two parameter dumps. A config in git plus a diff before flight shows what changed since last time.

CI and versioning of configs and code

Costmap

A costmap in Nav2: cells weighted by traversability, built from obstacles with an inflation margin. Paths are planned on it.

Navigation and obstacle avoidance: maps, planners, setpoints

Covariance

A measure of how two quantities vary together. The EKF covariance matrix describes how confident the filter is in each component of the state.

Probability and estimation: noise, Gaussians, fusion

craft_name

The craft name in the Betaflight config. A reliable drone identifier for scripts instead of a COM port number.

CLI automation over serial

CRC_EXTRA

A byte computed from the message definition and mixed into the MAVLink CRC. Catches dialect version mismatches between sender and receiver.

MAVLink internals: frame, CRC, dialects, signing

CRSF

The TBS Crossfire and ExpressLRS protocol: a regular UART, full duplex, channels and telemetry over a single port.

SBUS, CRSF and UART inversion

D-term

The derivative term of the PID. Sensitive to noise: with poor filtering it heats up the motors.

Filters

Damping ζ

A parameter of a second-order loop: ζ ≈ 0.7 gives ~5% overshoot; low ζ means bounce-back and ringing.

Control theory: stability, margins, cascades

DataFlash

ArduPilot's onboard binary log (.bin): VIBE, ATT, CTUN, BAT, ERR, XKF and hundreds of other messages.

ArduPilot logs: flight analysis

DCM

A simple complementary attitude filter in ArduPilot. A backup to the EKF; works without GPS and compass.

EKF and navigation

Dead reckoning

Dead reckoning from inertial sensors and wind without external measurements; accurate for seconds to tens of seconds.

Navigation and link resilience to interference

Density altitude

The altitude in the standard atmosphere with the same air density as right now. Heat and high elevation raise it, and props produce less thrust.

Weather and flight planning: wind, density altitude, cold, icing

diff all

A Betaflight CLI command that outputs only the differences of the configuration from the defaults. The unit for storing a config.

CLI and remap

DMA

Direct memory access: peripherals exchange data with memory without the CPU (DShot, SPI gyro, UART).

Embedded: interrupts, timers, DMA, RTOS

DRC

Design Rule Check: automatic check of a board for clearances, trace widths and vias against the fab's rules. Zero DRC errors is the condition for sending it to production.

PCB design in KiCad: from schematic to order, layout for EMC

DroneCAN

A protocol over CAN for autopilot peripherals: nodes with IDs, standard messages, 120 Ω terminators.

Connecting peripherals: UART, I2C, CAN in practice

DShot

A digital FC → ESC protocol: a 16-bit packet with a checksum, no calibration. The number is the speed in kbit/s.

Motors, ESCs and DShot

Dynamic notch

An automatic notch filter that finds peaks in the gyro spectrum and suppresses them.

Filters

EdgeTX

Open-source radio firmware (TX16S and others) with Lua scripts and an AUX port in Lua mode.

ESP32 and peripherals

EK3_SRC

EKF3 source sets (position, velocity, altitude, heading): GPS, baro, rangefinder, optical flow, external navigation; switchable in flight.

Altitude and position without GPS: baro, rangefinder, optical flow

EKF

Extended Kalman filter: fuses IMU, GPS, baro and compass into a single estimate of position, velocity and attitude.

EKF and navigation

Elevons

The two control surfaces of a flying wing that combine elevator and ailerons: deflected together for pitch, in opposite directions for roll.

Wing on INAV: mixer, auto launch, autotune

ELRS (ExpressLRS)

An open-source radio control system: choice of packet rate, telemetry ratio, 2.4 GHz and 900 MHz.

ExpressLRS: packet rate, telemetry, LQ

Engineering folder

Requirements with IDs, tests and a project changelog are a mandatory part of every capstone project.

Engineering Method 101: requirements, architecture, tests, documentation

Entry lag

Time from the start of a setpoint move until the gyro reaches 50% of it. The "sluggish on the sticks" metric.

Data-driven PID tuning

ESC

Electronic speed controller: commutates the phases of a brushless motor based on commands from the FC.

Motors, ESCs and DShot

ESP32

A microcontroller with Wi-Fi and BLE: peripherals, MAVLink bridge, radio companion.

ESP32 and peripherals

Euler method

The simplest numerical integration: x(t+dt) = x(t) + f(x)·dt. Unstable with a large step, hence the loop-rate requirements.

Differential equations and dynamics: from the motor to the loop

ExternalNav

External navigation source for EKF3 (VISION_POSITION_ESTIMATE/ODOMETRY), EK3_SRC* = 6.

Visual navigation: optical flow, VIO, SLAM overview

Failsafe

Behavior on link loss. In Betaflight: Stage 1 (guard time) and Stage 2 (DROP, AUTO-LAND, GPS_RESCUE).

Failsafe: stages and switch test

Fault injection

Deliberate injection of failures (motor, sensor) in SITL to test the behavior of the vehicle and the pilot.

Lua: scenarios and fault injection

Fault tolerance

The vehicle's ability to survive the failure of a single component (motor, BEC, receiver) and land under control. A quadcopter does not have it for motors; a hexa and an X8 can, given spare thrust, well-designed power and a firmware response verified in SITL or on a bench.

Onboard redundancy: X8, hexa, power and signals

Feedforward (FF)

A contribution to the motor command taken directly from stick movement, before any error appears. Speeds up the start of a move.

PID controller in Betaflight

Flight Review

PX4's web service for analyzing ULog logs: setpoint tracking, vibration, spectrograms and step-response estimation with no software to install.

PX4 control loops and tuning

FMEA

Failure Modes and Effects Analysis: failure → effect → detection → RPN → mitigation → verification.

Fault tolerance: failsafe, geofence, flight FMEA

Frame resonance

The frequency at which a frame or arm oscillates on its own. If it matches the motor speed frequency, vibration grows and the gyros get noisy.

Frame, mechanics and vibration

FRAME_CLASS / FRAME_TYPE

ArduPilot parameters: frame type (Quad=1, Hexa=2, Octa=3, OctaQuad=4, Tri=7) and layout (X=1, Plus=0…).

Introduction to ArduPilot

FSPL

Free-space signal loss: 20·log(d) + 20·log(f) + 32.44 dB; +6 dB per doubling of range.

Radio link budget and range

GCS

Ground control station: Mission Planner, QGroundControl, MAVProxy. Talks to the FC over MAVLink.

Tools: Configurator, GCS, git

Gerber

The set of board layer files (copper, solder mask, silkscreen, outline) plus a drill file: what the fab receives.

PCB design in KiCad: from schematic to order, layout for EMC

Gimbal lock

Loss of one degree of freedom in Euler angles at ±90° pitch: roll and yaw become the same axis. That is why quaternions are used internally.

Vectors, matrices and rotations: the language of attitude

Gimbal Manager

A role in the MAVLink Gimbal v2 protocol: the component that parses requests from the GCS and companion and commands the gimbal (Gimbal Device).

Gimbals and payload: MAVLink Gimbal v2, camera, drop

Glass-to-glass latency

Time from an event in front of the camera to its appearance on the screen; measured with a timer in the frame.

Cameras and video streaming on the companion

Ground loop

A closed loop through the common wire via two ground paths, where noise current is induced. Fixed by joining grounds at a single point.

Interference and electromagnetic compatibility

Group delay

The delay a filter adds to the useful signal; PT1 ≈ 1/(2π·fc).

Signals and spectra: what the gyro sees

GSD

Ground Sample Distance: how many centimeters of ground one image pixel covers. GSD = altitude · pixel size / focal length.

Mapping: surveying, georeferencing, photogrammetry

GUIDED

An ArduPilot mode in which position/velocity targets are set by an external source (GCS, companion).

Companion + ArduPilot: control from code

Gyro

A three-axis angular rate sensor. The main stabilization sensor, read thousands of times per second.

Flight controller and sensors

Hard iron / soft iron

Compass errors: a constant offset from magnetized parts (hard iron) and field distortion from ferromagnetic materials (soft iron); calibrated with offsets and a matrix.

IMU sensors: physics, errors, calibration

Harmonic notch (ArduPilot)

Harmonic notch filter INS_HNTCH_* with tracking by throttle, ESC telemetry or FFT.

ArduPilot tuning: AUTOTUNE, filters, ESC-based notch

HDOP

A multiplier on GNSS horizontal error due to satellite geometry; < 1 is excellent, > 2.5 is poor.

GNSS: how GPS works, errors, RTK

Heartbeat

A MAVLink message sent once per second by every participant: type, mode, armed state. No heartbeat, no link.

MAVLink and MAVSDK

Home

Copter's arming point. RTL returns here, not to the pilot.

Missions and planning

Image overlap

The fraction of shared area between adjacent frames along and across the route. For photogrammetry it is usually 70–80 % along and 60–70 % across.

Mapping: surveying, georeferencing, photogrammetry

Image-plane error

The target's offset from the image center (or a set point) in pixels or normalized coordinates. The outer tracking loop turns it into a velocity command.

Tracking and target-based control

INAV

A Cleanflight fork with navigation: position hold, RTH, missions, wings and rovers.

Introduction to INAV

Innovation

The difference between a measurement and the filter's prediction; if the normalized innovation exceeds the gate, the measurement is rejected.

The Kalman filter in plain terms, and the EKF

INT8 quantization

Converting network weights and activations from float to 8-bit integers. Speeds up inference 2–4× on NPU/TensorRT at the cost of a small accuracy loss; verified on a validation set.

Onboard ML: budget, quantization, evaluation, maintenance

Inverted signal

UART with inverted logic levels: idle is low. The SBUS standard; F4 can't invert in hardware.

SBUS, CRSF and UART inversion

Jetson Orin

A family of NVIDIA modules with an Ampere GPU (Orin NX/AGX also have DLA accelerators) for onboard inference. A typical companion for PX4 and ArduPilot.

PX4 + Jetson Orin: companion over UART, MAVLink and uXRCE-DDS

Jitter

Variation in a task's actual period; grows with locking and long interrupts.

Embedded: interrupts, timers, DMA, RTOS

Kalman gain

The weight given to a measurement versus the prediction, computed from the covariances; the adaptive counterpart of the complementary filter's K.

The Kalman filter in plain terms, and the EKF

Kt (torque constant)

Motor torque per amp of current, Kt = 60/(2π·KV) N·m/A; low KV = high torque per amp.

Motors, ESCs and DShot

KV

Motor RPM per volt with no load. Low KV for big props, high KV for small ones.

Motors, ESCs and DShot

L1 navigation

ArduPlane's guidance algorithm: the vehicle aims at a point on the route line a distance L1 ahead and commands lateral acceleration based on the angle to it. The NAVL1_PERIOD sets how aggressive it is.

ArduPlane tuning: wing AUTOTUNE, TECS, L1 navigation, landing

LANDING_TARGET

A MAVLink message the companion uses to send the autopilot the angular offset or position of a marker for precision landing.

Precision landing on a marker: ArUco, LANDING_TARGET, PLND

Lane switching

EKF switching between cores (per IMU) to a healthier one.

EKF and navigation

Lifecycle node

A ROS 2 node with explicit unconfigured → inactive → active states and transitions between them. Gives controlled startup and shutdown of an onboard application.

Architecture of an onboard ROS 2 application: nodes, lifecycle, watchdog

Link budget

The sum of power, antenna gains and losses minus FSPL versus receiver sensitivity; margin ≥ 10–15 dB.

Radio link budget and range

Logic condition (INAV)

A line of the INAV Programming Framework: a condition on flags and values whose result triggers an action or feeds other conditions.

INAV Programming Framework

Low-ESR capacitor

An electrolytic/polymer capacitor on the power bus that absorbs voltage spikes caused by wire inductance during ESC switching.

Onboard power circuit design

LQ (link quality)

Fraction of successful packets out of the last 100. RSSI is the power margin; you need both together.

ExpressLRS: packet rate, telemetry, LQ

Lua scripts (ArduPilot)

Onboard scripts: switches, sensors, scenarios. Require ≥2 MB of flash, do not run on F4.

Lua: scenarios and fault injection

MAVLink

The FC ↔ GCS/companion protocol: packets with a message ID, system_id, component_id and a CRC based on the XML definition.

MAVLink and MAVSDK

MAVLink dialect

XML description of a message set (common, ardupilotmega, development). Both sides must be built from the same dialect.

MAVLink internals: frame, CRC, dialects, signing

MAVLink microservice

A protocol on top of messages for one task: parameters, missions, commands, FTP. Defines the exchange order and retries on packet loss.

MAVLink microservices: commands, parameters, missions, streams

MAVLink signing

A MAVLink 2 mechanism: every packet is signed with a shared secret key and a timestamp. Rejects foreign commands and replays of intercepted ones.

Remote ID and vehicle security: broadcast, MAVLink signing, firmware protection

mavlink-router

A MAVLink router: one UART, many consumers over UDP/TCP.

Raspberry Pi + ArduPilot

MAVProxy

ArduPilot's command-line ground station in Python. Launched together with SITL.

SITL: a drone without a drone

MAVROS

A ROS package that bridges MAVLink to ROS topics and services. Superseded by uXRCE-DDS for PX4; for ArduPilot it remains alongside AP_DDS.

Libraries on top of MAVLink: pymavlink, MAVSDK, MAVROS: what to use and when

MAVSDK

A high-level SDK on top of MAVLink (C++, Python): arm(), takeoff(), telemetry subscriptions.

MAVLink and MAVSDK

Mission

A list of commands for AUTO mode: NAV (where to go), DO (what to do), CONDITION (when).

Missions and planning

Mixer

A table that converts throttle, roll, pitch and yaw into a command for each motor depending on the frame type.

How a multirotor flies

Moment of inertia

A measure of resistance to rotation: J = Σ m·r². Mass far from the center makes the vehicle sluggish in roll and pitch.

Flight mechanics: forces, moments, equations of motion

Natural frequency ωn

The frequency at which an undamped second-order system oscillates. Together with ζ it sets the settling time and overshoot.

Differential equations and dynamics: from the motor to the loop

Near miss

A near miss with no damage; the main source of improvements to the field protocol.

Field operations and teamwork

NED

North-East-Down coordinate frame: Z points down, climbing is negative Z.

Companion + ArduPilot: control from code

Node ID

A node's number on the DroneCAN bus (1–127). Set statically or assigned by the dynamic node allocation (DNA) server on the autopilot.

DroneCAN: the onboard CAN bus, nodes, ESCs and peripheral firmware

OBSTACLE_DISTANCE

A MAVLink message with 72 sectors of distances to obstacles around the vehicle. ArduPilot and PX4 use it to brake and avoid.

Depth and obstacle avoidance: sensors, OBSTACLE_DISTANCE, algorithms

OctaQuad (X8)

Four arms with two coaxial motors each. Fault tolerance at the cost of ~15% thrust loss.

Heavy-lift platforms: X8 and octo

Offboard (PX4)

Control mode from a companion with a setpoint stream ≥ 2 Hz; timeout COM_OF_LOSS_T.

PX4 and ROS 2: uXRCE-DDS and offboard

Ohm's law

U = I·R: the voltage across an element equals the current times the resistance. Power P = U·I.

Electricity and measurements 101

OpenIPC

Open-source firmware for IP cameras that turns them into a digital FPV transmitter over Wi-Fi adapters in packet injection mode.

Digital FPV video: DJI, Walksnail, HDZero, OpenIPC

Optical flow

The angular rate of the image moving under the camera; combined with rangefinder altitude and the gyro, it gives linear velocity.

Altitude and position without GPS: baro, rangefinder, optical flow

OSD

Telemetry overlaid on the video in the goggles: voltage, time, link, warnings.

Modes and OSD

Overshoot

Response overshoot past the setpoint after a step, in percent; related to damping.

Python for flight data analysis

Packet rate

The number of control packets per second. Higher means lower latency and worse sensitivity.

ExpressLRS: packet rate, telemetry, LQ

Phase margin

Degrees short of −180° at the loop's unity-gain frequency; delay eats into it linearly with frequency.

Control theory: stability, margins, cascades

PID

Proportional-integral-derivative controller: P reacts to the current error, I accumulates the steady-state error, D damps fast changes.

PID controller in Betaflight

Position hold

GPS position hold mode: the vehicle counters wind drift by itself. In INAV it is NAV POSHOLD.

GPS, compass and RTH in INAV

Pre-arm checks

ArduPilot checks before arming: calibrations, EKF, link, parameters. A PreArm message is a hint, not an obstacle.

Introduction to ArduPilot

Precision / Recall

Fraction of correct detections among those found / fraction found among the true ones; the confidence threshold trades one for the other.

Computer vision: from OpenCV to neural networks

Propwash

Shaking when the vehicle flies into its own turbulent downwash, usually during descent.

Data-driven PID tuning

Pure pursuit

"Pursuit" guidance: a point at lookahead distance L is chosen on the path, and the vehicle turns toward it with curvature 2·sin(η)/L. A larger L is smoother but cuts corners.

Guidance and trajectories: pure pursuit, L1, S-curves, minimum snap

pymavlink

Python MAVLink library: connection, reading messages, commands. Low level, everything visible.

MAVLink and MAVSDK

QoS

Quality of Service in ROS 2: reliability, queue depth, durability. A publisher and a subscriber with incompatible QoS don't connect, and there is no error: the subscriber simply receives nothing (the log only shows an "incompatible QoS" warning).

ROS 2 onboard: nodes, topics, QoS, tf2, bag

QuadPlane

VTOL in ArduPlane: takes off like a multirotor, flies like a plane.

QuadPlane and tilt-rotor VTOL

Quaternion

Four numbers with unit norm describing attitude without gimbal lock; the firmware standard.

Attitude: Euler angles, quaternions, complementary filter

Remote ID

Broadcasting the ID, coordinates and altitude of the vehicle and the pilot during flight (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi). A regulatory requirement in many countries.

Remote ID and vehicle security: broadcast, MAVLink signing, firmware protection

Resource remap

Reassigning a function (motor, UART, LED) to another board pad via the CLI.

CLI and remap

Root cause

The cause lies in the process, not in a person or a part: why the failure was possible at all. You eliminate it, or the crash repeats with a different part.

Analysis of real crashes

ros1_bridge

A bridge that forwards topics between ROS 1 and ROS 2 during a migration. Requires both distributions to be installed side by side.

From ROS 1 to ROS 2: reading old projects and porting them

Rotation matrix

An orthogonal 3×3 matrix with determinant 1 that transforms a vector from one coordinate frame to another. Its inverse equals its transpose.

Vectors, matrices and rotations: the language of attitude

RPM filter

A bank of narrow notch filters at each motor's rotation frequency and its harmonics, driven by bidirectional DShot data.

RPM filter: the case of the "whistling" 10"

RTK

Real-Time Kinematic: carrier-phase corrections from a base station give centimeter accuracy; requires a pair of dual-frequency receivers.

GNSS: how GPS works, errors, RTK

S-curve (jerk-limited)

A jerk-limited velocity profile: acceleration ramps up smoothly instead of stepping. This is how ArduPilot (WP_JERK) and PX4 build trajectories.

Guidance and trajectories: pure pursuit, L1, S-curves, minimum snap

SBUS

A receiver → FC protocol from Futaba/FrSky: 100 kbaud, 8E2, inverted, one-way.

SBUS, CRSF and UART inversion

Scheduler

The part of the firmware that runs tasks at a given rate and priority: gyro loop, PID, RX, OSD. The first thing you look for when reading the source.

How to read firmware source code: Betaflight and ArduPilot

SDF

Simulation Description Format: XML description of a Gazebo world and models: links, joints, sensors, plugins.

Simulation in Gazebo: worlds, sensors, a camera in ROS 2 and scenario tests

SERVOn_FUNCTION

The function of an FC output in ArduPilot: motor, tilt servo, control surface. A mismatch with the wiring is dangerous.

QuadPlane and tilt-rotor VTOL

Setpoint

The desired angular rate (from sticks and rates) that the PID compares the gyro readings against.

Blackbox

SiK

Open-source firmware for 433/868/915 MHz telemetry modems with frequency hopping; used by 3DR Radio and RFD900.

Telemetry radios: SiK, RFD900, ELRS MAVLink, IP radios and LTE

SITL

Software In The Loop: the real firmware running on a PC with a physics simulator in place of sensors.

SITL: a drone without a drone

Smoke stopper

A current limiter (bulb/resistor) in series with the positive lead at first power-up: a short circuit won't burn the board.

Onboard power circuit design

Spot welding

Spot welding nickel strip to 18650/21700 cells; soldering to cells is not allowed.

Li-Ion and LiFePO4 packs: cells, welding, BMS

Stability margin

The distance of the CG ahead of the neutral point as a fraction of MAC; 5–15% is normal.

Wing aerodynamics and plane flight modes

Stall speed

V_s = √(2mg/(ρ·S·C_Lmax)); in a turn it grows as 1/√cos φ.

Wing aerodynamics and plane flight modes

Strain relief

Securing a wire near a solder joint or connector so bending and vibration don't break the strands at the joint.

Soldering, harnesses and connection reliability

SWR

Standing wave ratio: the fraction of power reflected by the antenna back into the transmitter; 1.0 is ideal, ≤ 2 is acceptable.

Antennas, polarization and radiation patterns

System ID

The vehicle's number on a MAVLink network (1–255). Two vehicles on the same network must have different ones: in ArduPilot 4.7 the parameter is MAV_SYSID.

Multiple vehicles: addressing, separation, formation and a supervisor

systemd service

A way to start a script on a Linux companion automatically and restart it after a crash.

Raspberry Pi + ArduPilot

T/W (thrust-to-weight)

The ratio of the maximum thrust of all motors to the takeoff weight. 2:1 is the minimum for controlled flight.

How a multirotor flies

Target

A firmware variant for a specific board: MCU, gyro, pins.

Introduction to Betaflight

TECS

Total energy control for a wing: throttle sets the total energy, the elevator sets the split between altitude and airspeed.

Wing aerodynamics and plane flight modes

tf2

The ROS library for the tree of coordinate frames over time: map → odom → base_link → camera. Answers where the sensor was at the moment a frame was captured.

ROS 2 onboard: nodes, topics, QoS, tf2, bag

Thrust stand

Measuring a motor's thrust/current/power with a prop; checking the build sheet's calculations against reality.

Propeller aerodynamics and power system selection

Tilt-rotor

VTOL with tilting motors. Q_TILT_* parameters, TiltMotor* output functions.

QuadPlane and tilt-rotor VTOL

Traceability matrix

A requirement ↔ test ↔ result table; empty cells are open requirements.

Systems engineering: requirements, FMEA, documentation, tests

Troubleshooting tree

A "symptom → branch → measurement → cause" sequence of checks for finding a fault.

Troubleshooting tree: the drone won't take off

UART

Serial port: receiver, GPS, VTX, telemetry. Wires crossed over, common GND.

Flight controller and sensors

UART port (TX/RX)

Serial port: TX of one device goes to RX of the other. In Betaflight each UART is assigned a function on the Ports tab.

Ports and receiver

UDP broadcast / unicast

Ways of delivering MAVLink over a network: broadcast goes to everyone on the subnet, unicast goes to a single address. Over 4G/NAT and L3 VPNs (WireGuard, Tailscale, OpenVPN tun) only unicast works; broadcast only passes through L2 tunnels (ZeroTier, OpenVPN tap).

MAVLink routing and transport: radio, 4G, multiple GCSs

uORB

PX4's internal publish-subscribe message bus between modules. uXRCE-DDS exposes selected uORB topics to ROS 2.

Introduction to PX4

uXRCE-DDS

The PX4 bridge to ROS 2: a client in the firmware and the Micro XRCE-DDS Agent on the companion pass uORB topics as ROS 2 topics.

ROS 2 + PX4: uXRCE-DDS, an offboard node as a state machine

Vehicle log

A record of flight time, battery cycles, replacements and incidents for each vehicle. The basis for life-based scheduled maintenance.

Maintenance and reliability: logs, battery life, maintenance schedule, MTBF

VIBE

An ArduPilot log message with per-axis vibration and an accelerometer clipping counter. Below 30 is normal.

ArduPilot logs: flight analysis

VIO

Visual-inertial odometry: position and attitude from features in frames and the IMU; drift of 0.5–2% of distance traveled.

Visual navigation: optical flow, VIO, SLAM overview

VLOS / BVLOS

Visual Line of Sight: flying within the pilot's direct sight, the baseline requirement of most regulations. BVLOS (beyond visual line of sight) requires a separate authorization or category.

Rules, zones and operator responsibility

Voltage sag

The voltage drop under current across the battery's internal resistance: I × R.

Power: LiPo, Li-Ion, BEC

VTX

Video transmitter. Controlled by the FC over SmartAudio/Tramp/MSP according to the VTX table.

VTX tables and changing the video channel

vtxtable

The table of VTX bands, channels and power levels in Betaflight. FACTORY sends the channel number, CUSTOM sends the frequency.

VTX tables and changing the video channel

Waypoint

A mission waypoint: coordinates, altitude and an action (fly-through, loiter, RTH, landing).

INAV missions: waypoints and CLI wp

Weight budget

A table of the weights of all components versus the target takeoff weight. Determines the thrust-to-weight ratio and flight time before you buy anything.

Choosing components for the task and budget

Welch's method

Spectrum estimation by averaging FFTs over overlapping windows; resolution fs/nperseg.

Python for flight data analysis

Work queue (PX4)

Prioritized work queues in which most PX4 modules run on NuttX.
