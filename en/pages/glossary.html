Glossary

463 terms. Each one links to the module where it's explained in detail.

5-in-1 AIO

A micro board that combines the flight controller, four ESCs, a receiver and an analog VTX. It weighs 3–5 g; the price is few free UARTs, often no flash for Blackbox, and a failure of one part means replacing the whole board.

Whoops and micros: 1S/2S, AIO, SPI receivers and connectors

5V-tolerant input

A pin of a 3.3 V microcontroller that withstands 5 V on its input without damage. Most STM32 pins are, but not all (pins with analog functions are not); when in doubt, use a divider or a level shifter.

Connecting peripherals: UART, I2C, CAN in practice

`MAV_n_RATE` (MAVLink stream limit)

The outgoing stream limit of a MAVLink instance in PX4, in bytes per second, not baud; 0 means baud/20, half the bandwidth of an 8N1 port. If the sum of “size × rate” of the streams exceeds the limit, the rates of all non-constant messages are multiplied by (limit − constant) / scalable, within 0.05…1.

PX4 peripherals: GPS/compass, rangefinder, airspeed, telemetry, DroneCAN, OSD, LED and buzzer

`PARAM_MAP_RC` (binding a knob to a parameter)

The MAVLink message with which the GCS binds an RC_MAP_PARAM1…3 channel to a PX4 parameter: param = clamp(value0 + scale·rc, min, max), where rc is the knob's normalized value, recomputed no more than once a second. The binding is kept in RAM (rc_parameter_map) and lost on reboot, while the written parameter value is kept.

PX4: modes on switches, AUX channels and in-flight tuning (RC_MAP_PARAM)

`PWM_AUX_CENTn` (servo center, PX4 1.17)

The neutral of a PWM servo in microseconds on an FMU output; −1 means a straight line from MIN to MAX. If set, −1…0 maps to MIN…CENT and 0…+1 to CENT…MAX, and the firmware zeroes CA_SV_CSn_TRIM. Conversion: CENT = MIN + (MAX − MIN)/2 · (1 + TRIM).

PX4 outputs: functions, timers, servos, control surfaces, relays

`PWM_AUX_REV` (reverse mask)

A PX4 bit mask: bit n−1 flips the sign of output n's normalized value before conversion to microseconds, mirroring the range around (MIN + MAX)/2 or CENT. This is how servo mechanics are matched to the rule “positive command — trailing edge up” without touching the signs of CA_SV_CSn_TRQ_*.

PX4 outputs: functions, timers, servos, control surfaces, relays

`TRIG_MIN_INTERVA`

The minimum interval between shutter releases in the PX4 camera_trigger driver, in ms (default 1). An earlier release is skipped, not delayed: when shooting by distance a whole frame drops out and the spacing doubles. Set it to the camera's minimum cycle.

PX4: gimbal, camera trigger and geotags

A/B comparison (ABA, ABBA)

A comparison of two setups that differ in one parameter: A is the control, B the change, one flight program, several flights per arm. The ABA or ABBA order splits the drift of conditions (wind, heating, battery) almost evenly between the arms; AAAAA BBBBB blocks put all the drift on the difference.

The A/B method: tuning as an experiment

A2 CofC (A2 Certificate of Competency)

A pilot certificate for subcategory A2 of the Open category (EU, UK): on top of A1/A3, self-practice and an additional theory exam (in the EU with the authority, ≥ 75 %); valid for 5 years.

A career in drones: roles, skills, certificates, portfolio

Acro (Rate mode)

The Betaflight mode without self-leveling (ANGLE and HORIZON off): the stick sets the angular rate, and with the stick released the aircraft keeps its current tilt.

How to learn to fly: simulator, acro and the first hundred batteries

Action pinning by SHA

Referring to a GitHub Actions action by the full commit SHA (uses: actions/checkout@3d3c42e5… # v7.0.1) instead of a tag: the owner can move a tag to another commit, a SHA does not move. The version stays in a comment and is updated in a separate PR.

CI and versioning of configs and code

Actual rates

The Betaflight rate model (the default since 4.3): rc_rate×10 is the center sensitivity, srate×10 the maximum in °/s, expo changes only the shape of the curve between them.

Rates, RC smoothing and feedforward: from the stick to the setpoint

adjrange

A Betaflight CLI command for in-flight adjustments: adjrange <slot 0–29> 0 <range channel> <start> <end> <function> <adjustment channel> [<center> <scale>]. The function number is the position in defaultAdjustmentConfigs[] + 1; in 4.5 it differs from the enum from 25 on. Changes live in RAM until saved.

In-flight adjustments: the Adjustments tab, `adjrange` and the VTX on a switch

Adjustment slot

A place for one active adjrange function in INAV: 4 slots (0…3), 20 lines. An active line takes a free slot, an inactive one frees it if its function is in it; with two active lines the slot goes to the line with the lower index. One function cannot be put into one slot twice.

INAV in-flight adjustments: adjrange, stick commands, profiles, the OSD menu

ADR

Architecture Decision Record: context, options, decision, consequences.

Systems engineering: requirements, FMEA, documentation, tests

AGL (height above terrain)

Above Ground Level: height above the ground under the aircraft, h_AGL = h_AMSL − h_terrain. In a mission it is frame MAV_FRAME_GLOBAL_TERRAIN_ALT (10); ArduPilot takes the terrain height from the onboard database (TERRAIN_ENABLE) or a rangefinder. Not to be confused with height above the takeoff point.

Terrain following: terrain data, rangefinder, TERRAIN_*, safety

AHRS (AP_AHRS)

In ArduPilot, the front end of attitude, velocity and position estimation: every loop it picks the active backend (EKF3, DCM, EKF2, external AHRS, SIM) and hands its answer to the control loops; a change shows as the line “AHRS: EKF3 active” or “AHRS: DCM active”, and the selection parameter is AHRS_EKF_TYPE.

EKF3, EKF2 or DCM: how ArduPilot estimates attitude and position, and which to choose

Airbrake (a VTOL landing stage)

The QuadPlane landing stage in which the lift motors spin up as an air brake; it starts at a distance of V²/(2·Q_TRANS_DECEL) + 2V from the waypoint and lasts at least 1 s.

VTOL modes and transitions in ArduPilot

Airspeed envelope (FW_AIRSPD_*)

The four calibrated airspeeds of a PX4 plane: STALL, MIN, TRIM (cruise), MAX. In a turn PX4 raises MIN and STALL by √n, n = 1/cos(bank); with WEIGHT_BASE/WEIGHT_GROSS by a further √(GROSS/BASE). Starting values: STALL = V_s, MIN = 1.25 V_s, TRIM ≥ MIN·√n at FW_R_LIM.

PX4 from scratch: plane and VTOL

Airspeed scaling of the control surfaces (FW_ARSP_SCALE_EN)

A correction of the PX4 plane rate loop: s = FW_AIRSPD_TRIM / max(V, FW_AIRSPD_STALL); the P, I, D contribution is multiplied by s² and FF by s, because a surface's moment grows as V². Without a sensor in use, V = FW_AIRSPD_TRIM.

PX4 from scratch: plane and VTOL

Aliasing

Frequencies above fs/2 showing up at false frequencies after sampling; fixed with an anti-aliasing filter and full-rate logging.

Signals and spectra: what the gyro sees

AM32

Open-source firmware for 32-bit ARM ESCs (STM32, AT32, GD32, etc.), a replacement for BLHeli_32.

ESC firmware: Bluejay, AM32, BLHeli_32 and ESC configuration

Angle random walk (ARW)

The growth of angle error from gyro white noise: σ = N·√t, where N is the noise density in °/s/√Hz (0.005 °/s/√Hz = 0.3 °/√h). Over 2 minutes that is hundredths of a degree — attitude is spoiled by bias (b·t), not by noise.

Attitude: Euler angles, quaternions, complementary filter

Anti-gravity

A temporary boost of I (Ki + 0.34·AG) and P on roll and pitch during fast throttle changes in Betaflight: the throttle derivative, weighted toward low throttle, through a PT2 at anti_gravity_cutoff_hz and the anti_gravity_gain gain. Against the nose dip on punch-outs and chops.

PID controller in Betaflight

Antispark (precharge)

Charging the power bus capacitor through a resistor before the main contact closes: an XT90-S connector or a switch with a resistor. 10 Ω with 1040 µF gives 2.5 A instead of hundreds of amps, done in 5τ = 52 ms; without it every 6S connection loses ~0.33 J in a spark on the pins.

Onboard power circuit design

AP_DDS

The eProsima Micro XRCE-DDS client built into ArduPilot: publishes state to ROS 2 and accepts commands without MAVLink; on the ROS 2 side it is served by an agent (micro_ros_agent or Micro XRCE-DDS Agent).

ROS 2 + ArduPilot: AP_DDS and MAVROS, one node, two routes

AP_Notify

The ArduPilot library that shows the vehicle's state with LEDs, a buzzer and a display. The firmware code sets flags (armed, pre-arm checks, failsafe, EKF) and events, and the device drivers selected by the NTF_LED_TYPES and NTF_BUZZ_TYPES masks turn them into a color, a pattern or a tune 50 times per second.

LEDs and buzzer in ArduPilot: NTF_*, NeoPixel/ProfiLED, buzzer, DShot beacon

AP_Periph

ArduPilot firmware for DroneCAN peripheral nodes: GPS, compass, airspeed sensor, ESC adapter on a separate STM32.

DroneCAN: the onboard CAN bus, nodes, ESCs and peripheral firmware

Application rate

The volume of product per unit area, L/ha. Together with the swath width B and the speed v it sets the pump flow Q = q·B·v·60/10,000 L/min and the area per sortie S = V_tank/q.

Application segments: agriculture, inspection, delivery, shows

ArduPilot

An open-source autopilot: Copter, Plane, Rover, Sub. Navigation, missions, Lua, companion.

Introduction to ArduPilot

ArduPilot batch sampler (ISBH/ISBD)

Logging batches of raw IMU samples at the sensor rate for spectral analysis: INS_LOG_BAT_MASK selects the IMU, INS_LOG_BAT_CNT (1024 by default) the batch length. The ISBH header carries the type (0 — accelerometer, 1 — gyro), the multiplier mul and the rate; the value in rad/s = ISBD.x / ISBH.mul.

Python for flight data analysis

ArduPilot bootloader (.apj / _with_bl.hex)

A small program at the start of flash that accepts new firmware over USB. The first time, it is written together with the firmware as a _with_bl.hex file via DFU; after that you update with an .apj file from Mission Planner or QGroundControl.

ArduPilot from scratch: Copter with Mission Planner / QGC

Arming disable flags

Betaflight flags that prevent arming (RXLOSS, THROTTLE, ANGLE, MSP…). Visible via status and in the OSD.

Safety and the preflight check

ARSPD_RATIO

The airspeed sensor scale in ArduPilot: V = √(Δp × ARSPD_RATIO), where Δp is the differential pressure in pascals. The default is 2; after calibration it is usually 1.5–3.0. GPS correction: new = old × (V_GPS / V_indicated)²; ARSPD_AUTOCAL enables in-flight fitting.

ArduPlane from scratch: servos, control surfaces, airspeed, wing failsafe

AUTOTUNE

Automatic tuning of rate/angle gains from a series of "twitches" on an axis in hover.

ArduPilot tuning: AUTOTUNE, filters, ESC-based notch

Aux function (RCx_OPTION)

An action bound to a radio channel in ArduPilot with the RCx_OPTION parameter: a mode, arming, a relay, motor stop and so on. The channel is read as a three-position switch (LOW below 1200 µs, HIGH above 1800 µs), the function is called on a position change after a 200 ms delay and logged as AUXF.

Switches and knobs: RCx_OPTION, TUNE and in-flight tuning

BEC

An onboard voltage regulator (5/9/12 V) for the FC, receiver, camera and VTX.

Power: LiPo, Li-Ion, BEC

Bias

The sensor output at zero input; for a gyro it integrates into attitude drift; estimated by the EKF.

IMU sensors: physics, errors, calibration

Bidirectional DShot

DShot with a return channel: the ESC reports motor eRPM. The basis of the RPM filter.

Blackbox

Betaflight log: gyro, setpoint, PID, motors, RC at a high rate, for post-flight analysis.

Bluejay

Open-source firmware for ESCs on 8-bit EFM8 BB21 and BB51 (BB1 only up to version 0.18.1), the successor to BLHeli_S, with bidirectional DShot for the RPM filter.

BMS

Battery management system: voltage/current/temperature protection and balancing; cutting off discharge in flight is dangerous.

Li-Ion and LiFePO4 packs: cells, welding, BMS

BNF / PNP / RTF

Ways an aircraft is sold. BNF (bind-and-fly): built and set up, with a receiver; you bind your own radio. PNP: without a receiver. RTF: a kit with a radio, goggles and a charger; the radio and goggles of such a kit usually do not suit your next aircraft.

Bounce-back

Gyro rebound in the opposite direction after the stick stops; a metric of underdamping.

Data-driven PID tuning

BUILD KEY

The 32-character Betaflight cloud build key baked into the firmware (BUILD_KEY): the status command prints it as the line “BUILD KEY: …”; with it, build.betaflight.com/api/builds/<key>/json returns the list of build options. A local build has no key.

C rating

A battery's rated discharge current as a multiple of its capacity. Usually inflated; trust the log and the internal resistance.

Power: LiPo, Li-Ion, BEC

Callback group

A set of ROS 2 node callbacks with a concurrency rule: MutuallyExclusive runs them one at a time, Reentrant at the same time; the node's default group is MutuallyExclusive, so a MultiThreadedExecutor without your own groups gives no parallelism.

Architecture of an onboard ROS 2 application: nodes, lifecycle, watchdog

Camera matrix K

Camera intrinsics: focal lengths f_x, f_y in pixels and the principal point c_x, c_y. Maps a ray to a pixel and back; found by checkerboard calibration.

Camera geometry: from a pixel to a point on the ground

CAN arbitration

Resolving simultaneous transmission: a dominant 0 wins, the lower ID wins without losing time.

UART, I2C, SPI, CAN at the signal and driver level

CAN terminator

A 120 Ω resistor between CAN_H and CAN_L at each of the two ends of a CAN/DroneCAN bus; it suppresses reflections. On an unpowered bus a multimeter between H and L reads 60 Ω with correct termination and 120 Ω if one terminator is missing.

Connecting peripherals: UART, I2C, CAN in practice

Cascaded controller

Nested loops: position commands velocity, velocity commands angle, angle commands angular rate. The inner loop is tuned first and made several times faster than the outer one.

PX4 control loops and tuning

Channel forwarding

The Betaflight feature CHANNEL_FORWARDING: receiver AUX channels, starting from channel_forwarding_start, are output to the remaining servo outputs as is, without the min/max limits of the servo command.

Servos, PWM and timers: `resource SERVO`, `servo`, `smix`, SERVO_TILT and channel forwarding

Chirp

A test signal whose frequency rises smoothly from f₀ to f₁. The response to it is used to build the loop's frequency response.

System identification: chirp, frequency response, a model for tuning

Circuit breaker (CBRK_*)

A PX4 parameter that disables a check or protection only when an exact key number is written (for example CBRK_IO_SAFETY = 22027); protection against accidental disabling.

Circular polarization

The field rotates (RHCP/LHCP); less sensitive to orientation and reflections; never mix RHCP and LHCP.

Clamp meter

An instrument that measures current from the magnetic field around a wire without breaking the circuit. DC requires a Hall-effect clamp.

Measurements: multimeter, oscilloscope, logic analyzer

An INAV CLI mode that counts errors in pasted lines: diff and dump start their output with batch start, and if there is at least one ### ERROR line the first save does not write the configuration but prints the error count. It protects against saving a half-applied file.

CLOUD_BUILD

The flag a Betaflight cloud build is compiled with: it turns off the classic feature set of common_pre.h, so only what is in the options list goes into the firmware (DShot must also be listed explicitly). Locally it is set with OPTIONS="CLOUD_BUILD USE_…".

Coherence

A measure from 0 to 1 of how much of the output is linearly explained by the input at a given frequency. Below ~0.6, a point on the frequency response is not worth using.

System identification: chirp, frequency response, a model for tuning

colcon

The ROS 2 workspace build tool (replacing catkin_make / catkin build): colcon build --symlink-install, colcon test; the result is the build/, install/, log/ directories, the environment is source install/setup.bash.

From ROS 1 to ROS 2: reading old projects and porting them

Cold solder joint

A cold solder joint: dull, lumpy, with poor wetting. Holds on the bench and fails under vibration.

Soldering, harnesses and connection reliability

COMMAND_ACK

MAVLink command acknowledgment with a result. A command without an ACK check is hope, not control.

MAVLink and MAVSDK

Commutation timing (motor timing, advance)

How early the phases switch relative to the back-EMF zero crossing, in electrical degrees: the ESC commutes 30° − angle after the zero. Bluejay: steps 1–5 = 0 / 7.5 / 15 / 22.5 / 30° (default 4 = 22.5°); AM32: byte 10–42 = (byte − 10)·0.9375°. Higher gives more rpm and more margin against desync, but a hotter motor.

ESC firmware: Bluejay, AM32, BLHeli_32 and ESC configuration

Companion computer

An onboard computer for vision, scenarios, networking. It advises via GUIDED; the FC decides.

Companion: why, and where the boundary is

Complementary filter

Fusing the gyro (high frequencies) with the accelerometer/compass (low frequencies) using fixed coefficients; a special case of the Kalman filter.

Attitude: Euler angles, quaternions, complementary filter

Component ID

A component's number within a vehicle in MAVLink (autopilot 1, camera 100, gimbal 154, etc.). Together with the system ID it forms the address.

Your own MAVLink component: sensor, parameters, messages

Conducted interference

Noise conducted along power and ground wires (ripple from ESCs and converters), not through the air. Fixed with an LC filter and a low-ESR capacitor at the source.

Interference and electromagnetic compatibility

Config diff

A line-by-line comparison of two parameter dumps. A config in git plus a diff before flight shows what changed since last time.

CI and versioning of configs and code

Control allocation (CA_*)

The PX4 block that distributes the required moments and thrust among the actuators according to the given geometry: CA_ROTOR_COUNT, the CA_ROTORn_PX/PY positions and the CA_ROTORn_KM rotation direction.

PX4 from scratch: Vehicle Setup in QGroundControl

Control Derivative (mc_cd_*)

A term of the INAV multirotor controller proportional to the rate of change of the setpoint: kCD = mc_cd / 7270. The counterpart of feedforward in Betaflight: it speeds up the stick response and does not affect disturbance rejection.

Multirotor tuning in INAV: PIDs, rates, filters, EZ Tune and navigation PIDs

Control loop

A closed chain “measurement → controller → actuator → plant → measurement again”: the angular rate loop, the angle loop, the position loop; in a nested scheme the inner loop is faster than the outer one. A rate loop is the angular rate control loop.

Control theory: stability, margins, cascades

Control profile (control_profile)

One of three sets of PIDs, navigation gains, rates, TPA and EZ Tune in INAV (126 settings in 9.1.0). Selected with control_profile 1…3 (profile in INAV 7), by the battery profile (use_control_profile), or following the mixer profile (mixer_control_profile_linking). A plain diff prints only the active one.

Control surface throw

The angle a control surface deflects from neutral at full stick. On a wing it is measured with a ruler at the trailing edge: h = c·sin δ.

INAV from scratch: a flying wing

Copy-on-Write (pandas 3)

A pandas mode that is always on since version 3.0: any selection from a table behaves as a copy. The chain df[col][mask] = v does not change the original table (pandas raises ChainedAssignmentError); make the edit in one step: df.loc[mask, col] = v.

Python for flight data analysis

Costmap

A costmap in Nav2: cells weighted by traversability, built from obstacles with an inflation margin. Paths are planned on it.

Navigation and obstacle avoidance: maps, planners, setpoints

Covariance

A measure of how two quantities vary together. The EKF covariance matrix describes how confident the filter is in each component of the state.

Probability and estimation: noise, Gaussians, fusion

CPU limit (EdgeTX Lua)

The error with which EdgeTX stops a widget call that runs longer than ~20,000 Lua instructions (a hook every 200 instructions); the widget is disabled and the error text is written into its zone.

EdgeTX Lua widgets: your own telemetry screen

craft_name

The craft name in the Betaflight config. A reliable drone identifier for scripts instead of a COM port number.

CLI automation over serial

CRC_EXTRA

A byte computed from the message definition and mixed into the MAVLink CRC. Catches dialect version mismatches between sender and receiver.

MAVLink internals: frame, CRC, dialects, signing

CRSF

The TBS Crossfire and ExpressLRS protocol: a regular UART, full duplex, channels and telemetry over a single port.

SBUS, CRSF and UART inversion

Current shunt

A fraction-of-a-milliohm resistor in the power path; the current is found from the drop U = I·R amplified by an op-amp. 0.25 mΩ at 100 A gives 25 mV and 2.5 W of heat. Linear, but the amplifier's zero offset needs an offset calibration.

Measurements: multimeter, oscilloscope, logic analyzer

current_meter_offset

INAV current sensor zero offset; per the source, in units of 0.1 mV, it removes offset/scale amps from the reading. Calibrated against the idle current: new = old + (I_INAV − I_ammeter) × scale.

INAV from scratch: a GPS quadcopter

Custom Defines

The Expert Mode field in the Betaflight flasher: extra build flags separated by spaces without the USE_ prefix (the server adds it), for example RANGEFINDER, GPS_LAP_TIMER, TRANSPONDER.

Your own firmware builds

Custom OSD element (osd_custom_elements)

An INAV OSD element built by the user from three parts (text up to 15 characters, an icon, a number from a GVAR or a logic condition) with a visibility condition. INAV 8–9 has eight of them, numbered 147–149 and 154–158 in the layout.

OSD in INAV: analog and HD (DJI O3/O4, Walksnail, HDZero), layouts, alarms, custom elements

D-term

The derivative term of the PID. Sensitive to noise: with poor filtering it heats up the motors.

Filters

Damping ζ

A parameter of a second-order loop: ζ ≈ 0.7 gives ~5% overshoot; low ζ means bounce-back and ringing.

Control theory: stability, margins, cascades

DataFlash

ArduPilot's onboard binary log (.bin): VIBE, ATT, CTUN, BAT, ERR, XKF and hundreds of other messages.

ArduPilot logs: flight analysis

DCM

A simple complementary attitude filter in ArduPilot. A backup to the EKF; works without GPS and compass.

EKF and navigation

DDS_MAX_RETRY

An AP_DDS parameter: the number of agent pings in one batch (10 by default, 0 — no batches); after a failed batch the GCS gets “No ping response, exiting”, but in 4.7.2 the client keeps trying.

ROS 2 + ArduPilot: AP_DDS and MAVROS, one node, two routes

dds_topics.yaml

A file in src/modules/uxrce_dds_client of the PX4 firmware listing the uORB topics that the uXRCE-DDS client publishes to ROS 2 (/fmu/out) and receives (/fmu/in), with a rate_limit. A topic missing from the file is missing in ROS 2; the list changes only by rebuilding the firmware.

PX4 and ROS 2: uXRCE-DDS and offboard

Dead reckoning

Dead reckoning from inertial sensors and wind without external measurements; accurate for seconds to tens of seconds.

Navigation and link resilience to interference

Debounce

The time a new switch position must stay unchanged before the system reacts to it. In ArduPilot it is 200 ms for aux channels and the mode channel (SWITCH_DEBOUNCE_TIME_MS); a shorter pulse is not executed.

Switches and knobs: RCx_OPTION, TUNE and in-flight tuning

Demag compensation

Protection against desync: after commutation the current in the switched-off winding decays (demagnetization) and can hide the back-EMF zero. Bluejay computes a demag metric and above a threshold (Low 160, High 130) cuts power for a step; AM32 waits 1.25 intervals for a missed zero, coasts and recovers with power reduced by 1/32–1/8 (demag_comp 2–4).

ESC firmware: Bluejay, AM32, BLHeli_32 and ESC configuration

Density altitude

The altitude in the standard atmosphere with the same air density as right now. Heat and high elevation raise it, and props produce less thrust.

Weather and flight planning: wind, density altitude, cold, icing

diff all

A Betaflight CLI command that outputs only the differences of the configuration from the defaults. The unit for storing a config.

CLI and remap

Dijkstra (OA_TYPE = 2, ArduPilot)

The ArduPilot object avoidance planner for the geofence: it moves the vertices of exclusion polygons out by OA_MARGIN_MAX along the bisector and finds the shortest path on the visibility graph; it works in Auto, Guided and RTL only with the geofence enabled.

Navigation and obstacle avoidance: maps, planners, setpoints

Disk loading

Thrust divided by the total prop disk area, N/m². Hover power per unit of thrust grows as its square root: the larger the disk for the same mass, the more grams per watt.

Designing for a mission: spec → mass, energy, thrust, configuration

DMA

Direct memory access: peripherals exchange data with memory without the CPU (DShot, SPI gyro, UART).

Embedded: interrupts, timers, DMA, RTOS

DO_JUMP

Mission command 177: jump to command param1, param2 repeats (−1 — forever). A repeat count of N gives N + 1 passes of the segment.

Missions and planning

Drag polar

C_D as a function of C_L, usually C_D = C_D0 + k·C_L², k = 1/(π·e·AR). It gives the maximum lift-to-drag ratio, the maximum-range speed V_md and the minimum-power speed V_mp.

The plane: stability, CG, static margin, drag polar

DRC

Design Rule Check: automatic check of a board for clearances, trace widths and vias against the fab's rules. Zero DRC errors is the condition for sending it to production.

PCB design in KiCad: from schematic to order, layout for EMC

DroneCAN

A protocol over CAN for autopilot peripherals: nodes with IDs, standard messages, 120 Ω terminators.

Connecting peripherals: UART, I2C, CAN in practice

DShot

A digital FC → ESC protocol: a 16-bit packet with a checksum, no calibration. The number is the speed in kbit/s.

Motors, ESCs and DShot

DShot beacon

Motor beeping: Betaflight sends the ESC the DShot command BEACON1…5 (beeper_dshot_beacon_tone) on the RX_LOST and RX_SET conditions of the beacon command (both off by default in 4.5). Never while armed or with the motors running, no earlier than 1.2 s after disarming, no more often than ~0.5 s (RX_SET) or ~1 s (RX_LOST).

LEDs, the beeper and beacons: LED_STRIP, `led` / `color` / `mode_color`, beeper, DShot beacon

Duplicate RC packet

A packet in which the channel has not changed because the radio updates channels less often than the link transmits (for example the EdgeTX mixer at 4 ms on ELRS 500 Hz). Betaflight 4.5.3 substitutes the previous setpoint rate for the first duplicate and zero for the following ones; in 2025.12 this extrapolation is disabled for CRSF.

Rates, RC smoothing and feedforward: from the stick to the setpoint

Dynamic idle

A Betaflight controller that uses bidirectional DShot eRPM to keep the slowest motor at no less than dyn_idle_min_rpm × 100 rpm by raising the lower limit of all motors (up to 15 %, up to 5 % on the ground). With it, dshot_idle_value is ignored; without telemetry it is silently off.

The other Betaflight modes and features: turtle, launch control, 3D, acro trainer, Angle/Horizon, dynamic idle, camera control

Dynamic notch

An automatic notch filter that finds peaks in the gyro spectrum and suppresses them.

The Betaflight FLIP OVER AFTER CRASH mode (aux id 35): when arming with the mode on, the FC sends the ESCs DShot command 21 (reverse), and the stick spins the motors of one side backwards to flip the aircraft off its back. DShot only; to exit: disarm, switch off, arm again.

Two-point current sensor calibration

Finding a sensor's slope and offset from two or more currents measured with a clamp meter (a low and a high current). Unlike a calibration from one flight's mAh, it is accurate over the whole range: with a 50 mV sensor offset a one-point calibration is off by +74 % at 2 A, a two-point one by less than 0.5 %.

UDP broadcast / unicast

Ways of delivering MAVLink over a network: broadcast goes to everyone on the subnet, unicast goes to a single address. Over 4G/NAT and L3 VPNs (WireGuard, Tailscale, OpenVPN tun) only unicast works; broadcast only passes through L2 tunnels (ZeroTier, OpenVPN tap).

UERE (user equivalent range error)

The total error of one pseudorange (ionosphere, troposphere, orbit, multipath, receiver noise), usually a few meters for a single-frequency receiver. The horizontal position error ≈ HDOP × UERE.

UID (ExpressLRS)

The 6-byte address of an ELRS link: the first 6 bytes of the MD5 of the string -DMY_BINDING_PHRASE="phrase". Bytes 2–5 set the hopping seed, 4–5 the CRC seed; UID4 and UID5 are sent in the clear in the SYNC packet. Not encryption.

Unobservable degrees of freedom of VIO

User action (INAV mission action)

Bits 1–4 of a mission waypoint's p3 field (values 2, 4, 8, 16). They turn nothing on by themselves: logic conditions see them as operands 6–13 of the “mission” type — “action active on this leg” and “on the next one”. Not the same as the USER1–4 modes.

UWB (ultra-wideband beacons)

Ultra-Wideband: a tag on the aircraft measures ranges to fixed anchors by pulse time of flight, and the position is found by trilateration with 10–30 cm accuracy in line of sight. V-tail

A tail of two angled surfaces that together work as both the elevator and the rudder. In ArduPlane the halves are assigned the functions VTailLeft (79) and VTailRight (80); the firmware mixes the commands: left = (elevator + rudder) × MIXING_GAIN, right = (elevator − rudder) × MIXING_GAIN.

VIO

Virtual pitot (pitot_hardware = VIRTUAL)

An INAV airspeed estimate without a pressure sensor: GPS ground speed minus the component of the estimated wind along the nose, taking vertical speed into account. The wind estimate exists only on a plane; without it the full GPS speed is used. In 9.x the same estimate replaces a real pitot if its reading falls outside 30–200 % of it.

VPR (visual place recognition)

Coarse localization by image: every map tile is compressed into one global descriptor (NetVLAD, AnyLoc), and the frame looks for the k nearest. It answers “which tile does this look like”; only a following geometric check with local features gives a coordinate and confidence.

vtx (VTX on a switch)

The Betaflight CLI command vtx <slot 0–9> <aux> <band> <channel> <power> <start> <end>: sets the VTX band, channel and power index by the position of an AUX channel. Slots are checked on every RC frame and the slot that fired last time is skipped, so overlapping slots take turns overriding each other; band and channel change only until the first arm, power always.

WS2812 (addressable LED)

An LED with a built-in driver: 5 V, GND and one 800 kHz data line, 24 bits per LED (GRB), chained DIN → DOUT.
