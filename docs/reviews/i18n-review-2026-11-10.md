# Translation review: uk / en / uk-galych (sampled)

Reviewer: independent, read-only. Repository: `/home/claude/uav-education`. Date: 2026-11-10.
No repository file was modified.

## Scope and method

**Paragraph-by-paragraph comparison with the Russian source (theory + diagnostics), 12 modules, all tracks:**

| math | `math-prob-est` (full theory) | uk, en |

| tools | `ctl-sysid` | uk, en |

**Quiz banks (10), every question, correct option and distractors, ru/uk/en:** `bf-failsafe`, `ap-ekf`, `inav-gps`,
`px4-control`, `est-kalman`, `el-emi`, `link-elrs`, `ros2-px4`, `f-power`, `math-prob-est`. All 117 quiz banks were also
checked by script for `answer` index, option count, and key set in all three languages, and for duplicate options.

**Catalogs:** `ui.json` read in full for uk and en (681 keys). `data.json` (1739 keys) checked by script, plus a manual
read of every 6th entry (~290 entries). `diagrams.json` checked by script only.

**Whole-corpus scripts:**
- line alignment of every module against the source;
- numbers in each line compared with the source (thousands separators normalized);
- per-line length ratio, to find omissions and additions;
- Cyrillic left in `i18n/en` outside code;
- the Russian-only letters ы э ъ ё, and common Russian function words, in uk and uk-galych;
- second-person register («ви» pronouns and plural imperatives);
- consistency of 15 key terms.

### Corpus-wide results (scripts)

- **Structure:** every one of the 296 translated module files has exactly the same number of non-empty lines as its
  Russian source in all three languages. Sections, diagnostics markers, diagram directives and code blocks are intact.
- **Numbers/units:** no number was changed in any language. Only 15 lines differed, and all of them are
  thousands-separator formatting (`44 000` → `44,000`) or a word for a number ("в 2 раза" → «удвічі», "half").
- **Omissions/additions:**
  - **uk:** no length outliers.
  - **en:** 34 mild outliers, all checked, none an omission.
  - **uk-galych:** 30 lines are much longer than the uk line. Most of these are added flourishes (see the Galician
    section).
- **Cyrillic in en:** only `i18n/en/content/tasks/mav-protocol.json`, task `frame-len`. The parameter values
  `без подписи` / `с подписью` are internal keys used by the `answer` expression, and they are mapped to "unsigned" /
  "signed" through `labels`. This is by design, not a defect.
- **ы э ъ ё in uk / uk-galych prose and catalog values:** zero hits. The only Russian-looking words found were «уже»,
  which is valid Ukrainian, and plural "-и" suffixes (`UART-и`, `remap-и`).
- **Quiz structure:** 0 mismatches in `answer` index, option count or keys across 117 banks × 3 languages.
- **Galician catalogs:** `uk-galych/ui.json` (608/681 empty), `data.json` (1536/1739 empty) and `diagrams.json`
  (1299/1299 empty) are mostly empty. This is by design: `languages.json` declares `"fallback": "uk"`. The effect is
  that the Galician site inherits every uk catalog issue below.

## Ukrainian (`i18n/uk`)

**Overall:** very high quality. It is faithful, complete and terminologically disciplined. The sampled modules contain
no meaning errors. The main systematic problem is register: 27 files address the reader with «ви». In every case the
reason is that the Russian source itself uses «вы» there. The Galician version already rewrote them to «ти», so it can
serve as a reference for the fix.

### Register: «ви» instead of «ти» (glossary: «Звертання до читача — на «ти»»)

Severity: style, but it violates a binding glossary rule. Fix by switching every plural imperative and «ваш/вас/вам» to
the singular forms.

## Galician (`i18n/uk-galych`)

**Overall:** a successful, respectful experiment.
- **Dialect:** natural and of moderate density. It uses enclitic «ся» before the verb, «шо», «як» in the sense of
  «якщо», «зара», «заки», «нема», «лиш», «ніц», «гет», «фест», «пантрувати», «трафитися», «жеби/аби», «коло».
- **Readability:** a non-Galician Ukrainian reader can follow it.
- **Technical precision:** fully preserved:
  - terms, parameters, numbers and formulas are unchanged;
  - the diagnostic markers are intact;
  - the quiz answers are identical to uk;
  - «шруба» is never used for a propeller (only for a screw, or in a "like a screw into wood" simile).
- **Register:** consistently «ти»; it even fixed the uk «ви» files.

Problems:
- **One meaning change in a safety heading.**
- **A few phrases that a non-Galician reader will misread.**
- **A recurring added-flourish tic.** The project rule is «не додавати змісту», but the text adds remarks, mostly about
  «ґазда» (26× in 23 files) and «шуфляда» (11×). Each one is harmless, but together they drift toward caricature.

## Overall assessment

- **English:** publication-ready. One wrong UI search hint ("reversal") and two clarity nits.
- **Ukrainian:** publication-ready in substance. The terminology follows the glossary almost perfectly and no meaning
  errors were found in the sample. Before release, fix the register in 27 files (all inherited from «вы» in the
  source), plus «мультипаз» and the «видимість роботи» UI string. The «лозі/логу» and «варіація/дисперсія»
  inconsistencies are cheap to normalize.
- **Galician:**
  - **Strengths:** technically safe and pleasant to read. It is not a caricature: it reads like a mechanic from
    Lviv or Ternopil talking.
  - **Must fix:** the changed fire-procedure heading (G-1). Correct the three phrases a standard reader will misread
    (G-2, G-3, G-5) and the sentence-initial «Ся» (G-4).
  - **Recommended:** remove most of the ~16 added flourishes, especially those on safety lines (KILL, BVLOS, symptom
    markers), to honour the «не додавати змісту» rule and reduce the «ґазда» tic.
  - **Catalogs:** the Galician catalogs rely on the uk fallback, so every uk catalog fix also fixes gal.
- **Source:** fix S-1 (R vs Q) in all four languages; it is the only technical error found in this sample.
