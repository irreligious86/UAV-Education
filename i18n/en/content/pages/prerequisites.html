Prerequisites for Stage C and Beyond

The State Estimation, Autonomy, Engineer and Perception tracks assume you are comfortable with vectors and rotations, understand first- and second-order differential equations, and can compute a variance. Below is exactly what you need, where to get it in 2–4 weeks, and a 10-problem test that lets you decide for yourself whether to move straight on or take the Math Foundations elective first.

How to read your test result

  • 9–10 problems — go straight to the Stage C tracks; you can take parts of the elective as needed.
  • 6–8 — take the whole elective (three modules, ~13 h including labs).
  • fewer than 6 — the elective plus one external course in your weakest area, then take the test again. The numbers in the problems change on every page load.
